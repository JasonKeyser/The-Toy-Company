About Bizness Savvy Games

Bizness Savvy Games simulations are designed to go beyond the formulas and textbook definitions to build real intuition behind strategic business decision-making and how business performance links to the financial statements.

J6
J6:Y6

The simulations challenge the player to make decisions about how to allocate scarce capital among various opportunities in the face of uncertain business conditions – in short, the player learns to see the world as a real business manager does. The value of understanding the financial conclusions to be drawn from each data set becomes evident, as well as the nuances which may be lost in a business textbook or a test. These simulations reveal the interconnectedness between these analyses and the unpredictability of external factors, which can lead to a myriad of outcomes from the same decision.
