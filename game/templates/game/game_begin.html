🧸 The Toy Company

  • Are you savvy enough to run a toy factory?
  • Decide each year what toys to manufacture and how spend/invest company money.
  • Make it to a networth of $1 million in 30 years without going bankrupt and you win!
Toy Company Background

You are an ambitious twenty-year old who has just inherited the modest family toy factory. You do not wish to run the factory conservatively like your parents and grandparents - instead you want to grow the business massively, and make enough money to buy an oceanside property in the bahamas - but be careful; if you don't balance risk and reward properly you may run your business into the ground and squander everything.

You have a small runway of savings built up, and wish to make investments in equipment and infrastructure to operate more efficiently. You have fixed cost in the way of renting the factory. You will have to battle against capacity issues, as your factory has limited floor space for manufacturing, and of course the highly unpredictable demand for children's toys.

Win when you have reached enough money to retire in your Bahama Dream Property before you are too old to enjoy it!
